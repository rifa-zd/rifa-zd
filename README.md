I'm working toward becoming **Backend-focused Full Stack Developer**. Learning as I go, & **Cybersecurity** is turning out to be the most fascinating path.

My interest lies in the **thinking behind the systems**, what shaped the architecture — why it was built that way and **where it tends to break**. I favour learning from someone's experience that teaches the thinking behind it, **why, and what they'd do differently next time**.

Right now, I am mostly working with **Django + PostgreSQL**, but on my own I lean towards **Node.js, TypeScript**. I also try to keep up with *AtCoder* and at least one problem a day in **Leetcode** to sharpen my algorithmic skills.
